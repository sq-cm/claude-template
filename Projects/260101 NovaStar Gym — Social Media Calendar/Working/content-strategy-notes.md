# Content Strategy Notes — NovaStar Gym
**Strategist:** the Content Strategist  
**Date:** 2026-01-02

---

## Content Pillars (weighted)

| Pillar | Weekly frequency | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Member stories | 1x/week | Best on Tue or Wed — mid-week engagement peak |
| Education | 1–2x/week | Short-form tips perform better than long explainers |
| Community | 1x/week | Behind-the-scenes, staff, events |
| Offers | 1x/week max | Friday posts. Never lead with price — lead with outcome |

## Posting Rhythm

- **Monday:** Education (set the week's tone — useful, not salesy)
- **Tuesday:** Member story (social proof mid-week)
- **Wednesday:** Community or BTS (break up the feed)
- **Thursday:** Education or free-value content
- **Friday:** Offer or event promo (end-of-week action driver)

## Tone Guidelines

- Second person ("you") not third ("members")
- Short sentences. Max 3 lines before a line break.
- 1 emoji per post max in caption. Avoid in first line (affects reach on some feeds).
- Hashtag strategy: 5–8 tags. Mix: 2 broad (#gym #fitness), 2 niche (#brisbanegym #reformerpilates), 2 community (#novastarfam #yourgymbris)
- Never use: "crush it", "beast mode", "no excuses", "no pain no gain"

## Platform Notes

**Instagram:** Prioritise Reels for education content. Static images for member stories. Carousel for multi-tip education.  
**Facebook:** Repurpose Instagram content same day. Add 1–2 sentences of context (FB audience skews older, more context-seeking).
