## 1. Technical SEO — Score: 6/10

### Findings

**Crawlability**
- robots.txt present and correctly configured ✓
- XML sitemap exists at /sitemap.xml but includes 3 404 pages ✗
- Canonical tags present on most pages; missing on /menu and /order ✗

**Page Speed**
- Desktop: 74/100 (PageSpeed Insights)
- Mobile: 51/100 — primary concern
- Largest Contentful Paint: 4.2s (threshold: <2.5s) ✗
- Cause: unoptimised hero images (2–4MB each)

**HTTPS & Security**
- HTTPS active ✓
- No mixed content warnings ✓

**Mobile**
- Responsive layout ✓
- Tap targets adequate ✓

### Recommendations
1. Remove 404 pages from sitemap (quick win)
2. Compress hero images — target <400KB each
3. Add canonical tags to /menu and /order

## 2. On-Page SEO — Score: 5/10

### Findings

**Title Tags**
- Homepage: "Bloom Bakery" — too short, missing location keyword ✗
- Product pages: auto-generated by Squarespace ("Sourdough – Bloom Bakery") ✗
- No pages target "Melbourne" or suburb-level keywords

**Meta Descriptions**
- Homepage: missing ✗
- 8 of 12 product pages: missing ✗

**Headings**
- Homepage H1: "Welcome to Bloom" — not keyword-rich ✗
- /order page: no H1 at all ✗

**Internal Linking**
- Blog posts not linked from main nav or product pages
- No contextual links between related products

### Recommendations
1. Rewrite homepage title: "Bloom Bakery — Artisan Sourdough & Custom Cakes, Fitzroy"
2. Add meta descriptions to all key pages (template provided below)
3. Update /order H1 to "Order Artisan Bread & Cakes Online — Melbourne Pickup"
4. Add 2–3 internal links from blog posts to /order

**Meta description template:**
> [Product/service], handmade in Fitzroy, Melbourne. [Key differentiator]. Order online for pickup or visit us in-store.
