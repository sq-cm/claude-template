## Brand Positioning Statement

Thornwood Coffee is for people who want their morning cup to mean something — without being lectured about it. We put the drinker first. The origin, the farm, the roast — those earn their place in the story because they improve what's in the cup. Not the other way around.

**Positioning line (internal):** *Coffee that respects your intelligence.*

---

## Tone of Voice

**Primary attributes:**
- Direct — says what it means, no filler
- Confident without arrogance — earns trust, doesn't demand it
- Curious — interested in the person drinking, not just the product
- Tasmanian — understated, dry, real. Not Melbourne-hipster, not Sydney-corporate.

**What it sounds like:**
> Good coffee doesn't need a monologue. Here's what we roast, and why it tastes better.

**What it doesn't sound like:**
> We are deeply passionate about our craft and our journey to bring you the finest artisan beans.

---

## Differentiator

Most specialty roasters lead with origin (farm, altitude, process notes). Thornwood's differentiator is *usefulness* — the copy works for a beginner and a connoisseur without talking down to either. The Tasmanian identity is an asset, not a costume: it signals integrity and geographic separation from mainstream trends.
