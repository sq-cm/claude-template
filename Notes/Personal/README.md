# Personal

Private notes for your own use — thinking, drafts, reminders, and anything not meant for the team or a project.

## What goes here

- Personal to-dos, reminders, and scratch thinking
- Draft ideas before they become a project brief or a team request
- Anything you want kept out of shared team context

## Naming convention

No strict convention. Descriptive names preferred over dates:
```
[Topic or Purpose].md
```

## Structure

Flat folder. Add topic subfolders only if volume warrants it.

## Notes

- `Notes.md` is seeded here from the tracked sample `Resources/Onboarding/Notes.example.md` by whichever ran first on your clone — the SessionStart onboarding hook, `install.sh` / `install.bat`, or `/onboard` — a starting scratchpad, git-ignored and never overwritten once it exists
- Not a Deliverable and not team-facing — the Orchestrator does not route work here
- For daily journalling use `Notes/Daily/`; for shared team notes use `Notes/Team/`
