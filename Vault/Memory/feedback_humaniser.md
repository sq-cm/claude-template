Run `/humaniser` on every written content deliverable before marking complete — reverse briefs, proposals, copy, emails, reports, any client-facing or durable written artifact.

**Why:** AI writing patterns (pleasantries, negative parallelism, signposting, em dash overuse, "not an X" constructions) slip through even clean-looking drafts. Caught on Frosty Boy reverse brief — "Happy to jump on a call", three instances of "not an X" pattern, and signposting opener all needed fixing after initial draft.

**How to apply:** After any team member produces a written artifact, run humaniser before delivering to user or filing as final. Do not skip for "short" documents.
