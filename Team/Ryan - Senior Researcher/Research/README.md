# Research Briefs

Ryan's research briefs live here. Each brief is the foundation for a new team member's persona file. This folder is empty in a fresh instance — briefs are written during the hiring pipeline as roles are filled.

---

## Naming convention

```
[role]-brief.md
```

Examples: `seo-specialist-brief.md`, `webflow-developer-brief.md`

---

## Brief structure

Every brief Ryan writes must cover these sections, in order:

1. **Role Overview** — what this role actually does in a small AI-native studio context; what it is not; why it exists alongside the current team
2. **Core Competency Areas** — 4–6 areas with concrete sub-skills (not generic job-description language)
3. **What separates strong from average** — contrast pairs that define quality at this role; these become the persona's standards
4. **Guardrails** — what the role will and won't do; scope boundaries relative to existing team members
5. **Team Interactions** — how this role works with each relevant team member; who it receives from, who it hands off to, where boundaries sit
6. **AI Workflow Integration** — how the role uses AI tools natively; where AI accelerates work and where human judgment is irreplaceable
7. **Voice and Personality Traits** — 4–6 traits Harper uses to build a distinct, credible persona voice
8. **Recommended persona name and title** — Ryan's name suggestion for Harper

---

## Quality bar

A brief is ready for Harper when:
- Every guardrail names a *specific* existing team member whose domain it protects
- The "strong vs. average" section has at least 4 concrete contrast pairs
- The AI workflow section distinguishes acceleration from replacement
- The voice traits are distinct enough that Harper could not accidentally apply them to a different role

---

## Workflow

```
Sam identifies gap → Ryan researches → brief saved here → Harper builds persona → Sam announces hire
```

Ryan writes to Harper directly at the bottom of each brief: `Harper: use this brief to build the [Role] persona. Name them [Name].`

---

## Scope note

Briefs are working documents — inputs to Harper's persona build. Once a persona file exists and is active, the brief's job is done. Briefs are not updated to reflect persona evolution; the persona file is the living record.
