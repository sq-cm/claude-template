Search Platform Selection

Sample Client Co. needs a site-search solution for their product catalogue (approx. 80,000 SKUs). Three approaches evaluated against the criteria below.

  • Relevance quality
  • Integration complexity
  • Monthly cost at scale
  • Time-to-live
  • Ops overhead
  • Vendor lock-in risk

Approaches

Algolia

SaaS managed

Pros

  • Fastest time-to-live (< 1 week)
  • Excellent relevance out of the box
  • Comprehensive dashboard + analytics
  • React InstantSearch library

Cons

  • Highest monthly cost at 80k SKUs
  • Strong vendor lock-in on query DSL
  • Record limit pricing can surprise
Monthly cost
~$1,200/mo (Scale plan)
Integration
3–5 days
Timeline to live
1 week
Recommendation strength
65%

Typesense Cloud

Recommended

Pros

  • Open-source core — low lock-in risk
  • 3–4× cheaper than Algolia at scale
  • Comparable relevance for product search
  • Self-host option if budget tightens
  • Strong typo tolerance built-in

Cons

  • Smaller ecosystem than Algolia
  • ML-ranking less mature
  • Smaller community / fewer integrations
Monthly cost
~$320/mo (RAM-2 node)
Integration
5–8 days
Timeline to live
2 weeks
Recommendation strength
88%

Elasticsearch

Self-hosted

Pros

  • Maximum flexibility and control
  • No per-record pricing ceiling
  • Proven at very large catalogue scale

Cons

  • Highest ops overhead (cluster tuning)
  • Longest time-to-live (4–6 weeks)
  • Requires dedicated DevOps resource
  • Relevance tuning requires expertise

~$480/mo (EC2 + EBS)

Recommendation

Proceed with Typesense Cloud

Typesense Cloud delivers near-Algolia relevance at 27% of the cost, carries minimal vendor lock-in risk (the OSS core means a self-hosted migration path is viable), and hits the Q3 campaign deadline with a 2-week integration timeline vs. Elasticsearch's 5–6 weeks.

Algolia is the right answer if campaign launch were in two days — it isn't. Elasticsearch is appropriate only if Sample Client Co. expects catalogue growth beyond 1M SKUs and has dedicated ops capacity. Neither condition applies in v1.
