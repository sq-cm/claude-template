# Old patterns — cinema-director inheritance history (drops 2 and 3)

Record only. Nothing here changes output; the live rules are in SKILL.md.

**Drop 2, applied 04/08/2026.** Ripple fold from upstream drop 2 (cinema-director rebuild): retired the old two-letter acronym and the skill's former name for its sibling, replaced throughout with `cinema-director`; reconciled every "shared with the sibling skill" / "same as the sibling skill" claim against cinema-director's new thirteen-block locked house format. Where cinema-director still carried an identically-named block (Subject Lock, Cross-Frame Rules, Last Frame, World Plate, Sound Bed, Element Tags, FOV Degree Table, Write The Visible, House Rules), the inheritance claim stood with an updated section pointer. Where a block no longer existed standalone upstream (Scene & Mood, Frame Map, Movement, the old separate Capture Realism / Camera Capture, the pre-prompt-confirmation workflow, the Distributed Style heading, the Cuts & Timing Precision Scale, the four-mechanic Capture Realism structure), this skill marked that content as its own retained legacy grammar and pointed to the closest analogous new section rather than claiming direct inheritance.

**Drop 3, applied 19/08/2026.** Cinema-director was base-flipped onto upstream drop 3 and its spine moved from thirteen blocks to a sixteen-slot order, gated by a Seedance 2.0-vs-2.5 target-version check. This skill adopted no upstream drop-3 content — its twelve-block structure is unchanged — but every inheritance claim was re-reconciled per block, not renamed in bulk, because the slots are not a relabelling of the old blocks:

- **Survive under the same name** — Element Tags, Write The Visible, House Rules.
- **Survive under a new name** — FOV Degree Table → § SLOT 8 — OPTICS (§ FOV degree anchor); Sound Bed → § SLOT 15 — AUDIO; Frame Map → § SLOT 6 — GEOMETRY MAP. Frame Map is a reversal of the drop-2 finding: cinema-director had no standalone screen-space block under the thirteen-block spine, and drop 3 reinstated one, so Frame Map moved back into shared grammar.
- **Reshaped, not renamed** — Subject Lock was merged with Prop Lock and Corps/Crew Lock into § SLOT 5 — ASSETS (`@tag = identity + THIS SCENE action + fidelity assertion`); Cross-Frame Rules became § SLOT 16 — LOCKS, restated as a positive ordered chain. Both retained here as this skill's own legacy grammar; the "identical in grammar, logic, and rules" claim no longer holds for either.
- **No longer standalone** — World Plate folded into the SLOT 5 location asset line; Movement's four-layer structure and cinema-director's former § MOVEMENT AND CHOREOGRAPHY heading are both gone (analogues: § SLOT 12 — ACTION TIMING, § SLOT 9 — CAMERA).
- **Deleted upstream with no successor** — Last Frame. Cinema-director now opens on § SLOT 7 — FIRST FRAME.
- **Reversed outright** — distributed style (below).

**Per-section history moved out of the body:**

- **Block lineage.** This skill's twelve-block structure descends from cinema-director's original ten-block grammar; cinema-director moved to a thirteen-block house format at drop 2 and a sixteen-slot spine at drop 3, so the two skills have not shared an identical block list since.

Its anti-redundancy discipline survives as the § LENGTH DISCIPLINE "every fact lives in exactly one slot" rule, which this skill's per-block placement already satisfies.

- **Delivery.** Cinema-director's delivery expanded to three parts (title, tag list, code block — § DELIVERY). This skill's old numbered bullet reference list was retired in favour of element tags.
- **Universal rules.** Descended from cinema-director's grammar. Cinema-director's rules are no longer numbered, so the "Rule 11" citation was retired.
- **Cuts.** Cinema-director's § CUTS & TIMING PRECISION SCALE heading is gone; its closest grammar is the four-tier energy dial in § SLOT 9 — CAMERA ("Dynamic register") plus the per-shot timecoding, cut policy and runtime guide in § SLOT 1 — HEADER.

- **Reference ceiling.** Became version-conditional at drop 3.
- **Audio.** Sound Bed became § SLOT 15 — AUDIO at drop 3, which also promoted a `NO BGM` convention into cinema-director's header block on silent scenes.
- **Capture Realism.** Cinema-director merged its Capture Realism content into a single closing block at drop 2, then dissolved it at drop 3 across § SLOT 2 — STYLE PREFIX, § SLOT 8 — OPTICS, § SLOT 9 — CAMERA, § SLOT 10 — LIGHT & COLOUR and § SLOT 16 — LOCKS. Its § CORE PHILOSOPHY heading and the "feels captured, not rendered" phrasing went at the flip.
- **Atmosphere.** Cinema-director promoted atmosphere to a full slot (§ SLOT 11 — ATMOSPHERE) and made it source-bound at drop 3.
- **Moisture.** Cinema-director's grammar no longer carries a moisture mechanic in any form.
- **Contrast curve.** Cinema-director § SLOT 10 — LIGHT & COLOUR added a 70/20/10 colour-accent doctrine at drop 3 but names no three-way contrast-curve mechanic.
