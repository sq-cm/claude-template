Been using something for the past few weeks and wanted to share it before it's obvious.

It's a Claude Code workspace called the AI Team Template. The idea: instead of one AI assistant you prompt yourself, you get 20 specialists — SEO, copy, UX, analytics, social, dev, brand — coordinated by an orchestrator named Sam. You talk to Sam, he routes the work to whoever should handle it.

In practice: briefing documents you don't have to write yourself. First drafts that come back structured. Audits that actually surface something. Research that's done before you've finished your coffee.

To try it:
1. Download the `Claude - TEMPLATE` folder
2. Open it in Claude Code
3. Type: "Hi Sam, what can the team help me with today?"

That's it. No setup beyond that. Give it one real task — something from your actual queue — and see what comes back.
