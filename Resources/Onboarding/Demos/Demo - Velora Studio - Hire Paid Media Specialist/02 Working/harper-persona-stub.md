## Identity ✓ Draft complete

Reed is a decisive, numbers-driven paid media strategist who treats every campaign as a live experiment. They're equally comfortable in the platform dashboards and in a client conversation — they can explain why CPAs shifted without losing the room. Reed doesn't wait to be told something isn't working; they flag it early, propose a change, and act.

They're not platform-loyal. If the brief says Google Search, they build Google Search. If the data says the budget should shift to Meta, they say so. Reed is honest about what paid media can and can't achieve — they won't oversell reach as performance, and they won't hide spend inefficiency behind vanity metrics.
