# Client Brief — Thornwood Coffee

**Client:** Thornwood Coffee Roasters
**Website:** thornwoodcoffee.com.au *(fictional)*
**Date:** 2026-01-01
**Prepared by:** the Orchestrator (routed from client intake)

---

## Business Overview

Thornwood is a specialty coffee roaster based in Hobart. They source single-origin beans direct from farms, roast in small batches, and sell via their own website and through 12 wholesale café partners across Tasmania and Victoria.

## Goals

- Launch a refreshed brand voice for a website redesign (Q1 2026)
- Hero copy for the homepage — needs to work for both direct-to-consumer and wholesale audiences
- Tone: distinctive enough to stand out from the sea of generic "craft" coffee brands

## Current Situation

- Current website copy is neutral and functional — "quality beans, shipped to your door"
- New brand direction: lean into Thornwood's Tasmanian identity and the relationship with farmers
- Biggest competitor is swamped with "origin story" copy — Thornwood wants to lead with *the drinker*, not the brand

## Deliverable Requested

6 hero headline options for the homepage. Each headline: max 8 words. Accompanied by a 1-sentence subheadline (max 20 words). The Brand Strategist to provide brand positioning context first, then the Copywriter to write the headlines.

## Audience

Two groups:
1. **Home brewers** — enthusiasts, aged 28–45, already drink specialty coffee, care about origin and process
2. **Café buyers** — procurement decision-makers, care about consistency, relationship, and margin

## Voice Constraints

- Not precious or try-hard
- Dry wit acceptable; outright humour only if it lands
- No: "craft", "artisan", "passion", "journey", "story"
- Allowed: direct address, imperative, questions, silence (short copy can be powerful)

## Timeline

Positioning brief → copy draft → delivered within 3 business days.
