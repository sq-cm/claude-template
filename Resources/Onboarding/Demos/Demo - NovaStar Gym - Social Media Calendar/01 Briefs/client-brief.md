# Client Brief — NovaStar Gym

**Client:** NovaStar Gym  
**Platforms:** Instagram, Facebook  
**Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Prepared by:** the Orchestrator (routed from client intake)

---

## Business Overview

NovaStar is a mid-size commercial gym in Brisbane. 1,200 members. Mix of serious lifters and casual fitness people. Recently added group classes (HIIT, yoga, reformer pilates).

## Goals

- Grow Instagram following from 3,400 to 5,000 by mid-year
- Drive class bookings via social (current: ~15/week from social referrals)
- Build community feel — less "gym promo", more "lifestyle and people"

## Content Pillars (from strategy session)

1. **Member stories** — real members, real results, authentic voices
2. **Education** — training tips, nutrition basics, "why this works" content
3. **Community** — behind-the-scenes, staff spotlights, event promos
4. **Offers** — promotions, class schedule, new member drives (max 1 per week)

## Tone

Energetic but not aggressive. Inclusive. Occasional humour. Anti-bro-culture. Appeals to both the 25-year-old lifter and the 45-year-old starting their fitness journey.

## Deliverable Requested

4-week social media content calendar. 5 posts per week (Mon–Fri). Each post: platform, content pillar, caption copy, hashtag set, visual prompt for image generation.

## Timeline

Calendar needed by end of Week 1 for client sign-off before scheduling.
