# Scratchpad

---

## 📥 Inbox

*Only if it doesn't clearly fit Todo, Idea, Prompt, or Handoff: genuinely unsure, not just unsorted. New items go at the top.*

---

## ✅ Todo

*Has a concrete next action you could do right now? Todo. Still a "maybe" with no action yet? That's an Idea.*

- [ ] Sample task

---

## 🧩 Prompts

*Save reusable prompt drafts here, each written to the vault's 5-slot formula: Context, Goal, Insight, Output spec, Constraints/anti-goals + a Done when line.*


---

## 🤝 Handoffs

*Stash context here to hand off to a teammate or your next session — what's done, what's left, and where things live.*


---

## 💡 Ideas

*A thought worth keeping with no action attached yet. The moment it needs a checkbox, move it to Todo. One line per entry; new items at the top.*

---

## 📦 Archive

*Cut-paste only — never reorganise.*
