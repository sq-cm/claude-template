# Personal

Your private inbox. Use it for thinking, drafts, reminders and anything that isn't meant for the team or a project yet.

## What goes here

- Personal to-dos, reminders and scratch thinking
- Draft ideas before they become a project brief or a team request
- Material you've captured but haven't routed anywhere yet
- Anything you want kept out of shared team context

## Naming convention

No strict convention. Descriptive names work better than dates:
```
[Topic or Purpose].md
```

## Structure

Flat folder. Add topic subfolders only if volume warrants it.

## Notes

- `Notes.md` is seeded here from the tracked sample `Resources/Onboarding/Notes.example.md` by whichever runs first on your clone: the SessionStart onboarding hook, `install.sh` / `install.bat`, or `/onboard`. It's a starting scratchpad, git-ignored and never overwritten once it exists
- Not a Deliverable and not team-facing. The Orchestrator doesn't route work here
- For shared team notes use `Inbox/Team/`
